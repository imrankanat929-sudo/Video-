What if people could read each other's minds?
Imagine waking up one morning and realizing... that now you can hear other people's thoughts.
You look at your parents, and you hear their inner voice.
You step outside, and suddenly you begin to understand what complete strangers are thinking.
At first, it feels like a real superpower. But very quickly, you realize one thing.
You can't turn it off.
Day one. You go to school.
The teacher is explaining a new topic, but instead of his words, your head is filled with your classmates' thoughts.
One is thinking about the exam. Another dreams of going home as soon as possible.
A third keeps replaying yesterday's conversation.
You try to focus, but there are hundreds of voices around you.
And suddenly, you hear a thought that makes you freeze.
"I hope he never finds out what I really think of him."
Day three. The whole world already knows about this ability.
People start avoiding conversations.
Friends fight over thoughts that no one would ever have heard before.
Even an ordinary smile no longer means that a person is truly happy to see you.
Thousands of videos appear online, from people talking about their unusual abilities.
But no one knows why it happened.
Day five. The world becomes completely different.
Imagine that it's impossible to hide your true intentions.
Politicians can't hide their thoughts.
Sellers know what you want to buy. And your friends hear even the things you would never dare to say.
It seems like now everyone will be honest. But along with the lies, privacy disappears too.
After all, not every thought should be known to others.
Day seven. You wake up and notice that for the first time in a week, there is complete silence.
No more voices. No more thoughts.
The ability is gone.
But when you meet your best friend, you hear a voice again, right inside your head.
"I know you've been hearing my thoughts this whole time, too."
You look at him. He smiles.
And then you realize... maybe this ability was no accident.
Now answer me this. If you could read the thoughts of only one person, who would you choose?
And most importantly... would you want to know what that person thinks about you?
