# DIDAO — Season 1: THE FOREST HAS NO EXIT (6 episodes)

Episode 1 is produced. Episodes 2–6 below are an outline for you to approve or change; I wrote them, they weren't in the brief.

| # | Title | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Last Night** ✅ | Camping trip; a tall faceless figure in the trees; footsteps at midnight; the path vanishes; "DO NOT LOOK BACK"; "I CAN SEE YOU ALL"; three figures, one much closer; "where is the fourth?" — the tent is empty and **Max is gone**. |
| 2 | Don't Turn Around | The hero and Lena search for Max without looking back. Every time one of them glances behind, the trees change. |
| 3 | The Carvings | They find more carved trees — older warnings from someone who was here before. A map scratched into bark. |
| 4 | The One Who Stayed | An abandoned camp with a teenager's diary. The last page is dated tomorrow. |
| 5 | Signal | One bar of signal on a hill. The call connects… to their own voices from the first night. |
| 6 | No Exit | The edge of the forest. The hero must choose: leave alone, or go back for Max. Cliff-hanger into Season 2. |

**Continuity rules for every episode:** same hero rig, same forest seed and layout, same palettes, same post-processing
(`core.post`), same monster design. New locations are added to `forest.py` and documented here, never generated ad hoc.

**Narration style (from Episode 1):** second person ("you…"), client-recorded RU + EN voiceover; each episode ends with a question to the viewer.
