Imagine waking up one morning.

The first thing you want to do is drink a glass of cold water.

You walk into the kitchen, grab a glass, turn on the tap, but nothing happens.

You try again.

You turn the handle harder, still nothing.

That's strange.

Maybe they've shut off the water, you think.

You decide to wash your face instead.

You walk into the bathroom, turn on another tap, and even try the shower.

But the result is the same.

Not a single drop.

You grab your phone and check your messages.

Maybe there's a problem with the building, but there are no warnings.

Then you walk over to the window, and that's when things get terrifying.

You live near a river.

Every morning, you can see boats, hear the flowing water, and watch people walking along the riverbank.

But today, the river is gone.

In its place is nothing but a massive stretch of dry land.

You think it must be some kind of mistake.

Maybe there's been an accident at a dam.

But then you look further.

The fountains aren't working.

The lake and the park has disappeared.

Every small body of water around the city has turned into an empty hole.

You turn on the television.

Breaking news appears on the screen.

The reporter explains that the same situation is happening in countries all around the world.

At first, people thought it was just a problem with the water supply.

But soon, they discovered something impossible.

The water hadn't just disappeared from the taps.

It had disappeared from rivers, lakes, seas, and oceans.

Imagine this.

Enormous ships are now lying on completely dry ocean floors.

Marine animals have lost their natural habitats.

Entire cities are beginning to panic.

People rush into stores, desperately searching for even a few bottles of water.

But supplies are running out quickly.

You realize that the glass of water you took for granted yesterday has suddenly become incredibly valuable.

A few hours pass.

You walk outside and see people standing in the middle of the dried-up river.

Some are taking pictures.

Others are simply staring in disbelief.

But then you notice something strange.

In the middle of the dry riverbed, there's a metal door.

You walk closer.

The door is slightly open.

Suddenly, you hear three loud knocks coming from the darkness.

You take out your phone, turn on the flashlight, and point it inside.

At that exact moment, an emergency notification appears on your screen.

It says, Do not try to bring the water back.

You freeze.

Who sent that message?

And why is someone hiding underground?

And most importantly, what if the disappearance of water was only the beginning?
