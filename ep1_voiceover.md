## Plain text (EN)

I was the only person moving in a city full of frozen people.
Cars stood in the middle of the road.
A bird hung motionless above a rooftop.
Even the water in the fountain had stopped in midair.
But the strangest thing was waiting inside my apartment.
I woke up on an ordinary Tuesday and checked my phone.
It said nine seventeen.
The clock on the wall showed the same time, but its second hand was not moving.
I looked through the window.
A bus was frozen halfway through a turn.
People stood on the pavement like statues.
One man's keys were hanging above the ground.
I ran downstairs and shouted for help.
Nobody answered.
I waved my hand in front of a woman's face.
Nothing.
Then every phone on the street lit up at once.
Mine buzzed too.
A message appeared from an unknown sender:
“YOU HAVE TEN MINUTES.”
Ten minutes until what?
I looked up at the city clock.
Its hands began to move, even though every other clock was still.
A second message arrived.
“DO NOT GO HOME.”
Behind me, the apartment building door slowly opened by itself.
Would you go inside to find out who sent the message, or run toward the clock tower?
