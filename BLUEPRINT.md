# Leather Brand Launch Blueprint

Premium leather bags for students and young professionals, run by a 14-year-old founder.
Products: backpacks, messenger bags, minimalist cardholders, duffels.
Channels: TikTok, Instagram Reels, Telegram/WhatsApp catalog, landing page → Shopify later.

Working brand name used in this doc and the code: **HIDE & HUSTLE** (swap it for your own).

---

## 0. Ground rules for a 14-year-old founder (read first)

These are not optional. They protect you, your parents and your customers.

| Area | What the rule is | Your workaround |
|---|---|---|
| Store & payments | Shopify, Stripe, PayPal, TikTok Shop and most banks require the account holder to be 18+. | A parent/guardian opens the accounts **in their name** and is the legal owner. You run the work day to day. Write this down as a simple family agreement (who owns what, how profit is split, how much goes to savings). |
| Business registration & tax | Rules differ by country. Usually a parent can register as a sole trader / self-employed, or you sell as a "hobby" up to a small threshold. | Before sale #1, a parent spends 30 minutes on your country's tax-office website (or with an accountant) to find the threshold and what records to keep. Keep every receipt from day 1 (spreadsheet + photo of receipts). |
| Social media | TikTok and Instagram allow 13+, but TikTok LIVE gifts, TikTok Shop and creator payouts are 18+. Accounts of under-16s are private by default on TikTok. | Run the **brand account** from a parent-owned business account. Your face can be in videos; your personal account stays separate. |
| Safety | You will get DMs from strangers. | Never post your school name, home address, street views from your window, or your full schedule. Ship from a parent's address or a post-office box. Use a business email and a separate phone number/WhatsApp Business for orders. A parent can read the order inbox. |
| Honest labeling | "Genuine leather" is legally a *lower* grade in many markets (it is real leather, but not full-grain or top-grain). | Label exactly what you sell: **full-grain**, **top-grain**, or **genuine (split) leather**. Ask the supplier for a written spec, and do a burn/smell/water test on a sample. Never call PU leather "leather". |
| Refunds | Customers in most countries have legal rights to return online purchases (often 14 days in the EU/UK). | Publish a clear policy: 14-day returns, buyer pays return shipping unless the item is faulty. |

## 1. Brand Strategy & Product Positioning

### 1.1 Positioning statement

> For students and young professionals who want a bag that looks expensive, lasts for years and doesn't cost a month's rent, HIDE & HUSTLE makes minimalist full-grain leather bags — designed, checked and packed by a 14-year-old founder who answers every message personally.

### 1.2 The USP: "Why buy from a 14-year-old?"

Don't hide your age. Turn it into the reason to buy. Customers buy from you for three reasons that a big brand can't copy:

1. **Radical transparency.** You show everything: the supplier calls, the leather samples you rejected, the cost breakdown, the mistakes. Big brands can't show their margins; you can. *"This cardholder costs me $7.40 to make. I sell it for $29. Here's where every dollar goes."*
2. **Personal accountability.** Every bag is checked and packed by you, with a handwritten note signed by name. You reply to every DM. If something goes wrong, the founder fixes it — not a call centre.
3. **You're buying into a story.** People love backing a young founder. Each order is visibly "helping a 14-year-old build something real". Customers become fans and fans share.

### 1.3 Trust-builders (the trust stack)

Do all of these before you spend $1 on ads:

- **Leather proof:** a 10-second video of the burn/water test and a close-up of the grain on every product page. Spec card: leather type, thickness (e.g. 1.4 mm), stitching (waxed thread), hardware (brass/zinc).
- **Lifetime stitching guarantee** on cardholders, **1-year guarantee** on bags. Costs little (stitching failures are rare) and removes risk for the buyer.
- **14-day return promise** in plain words on the site.
- **Real faces:** you on camera + a parent occasionally visible ("my mom handles the bank, I handle everything else") — this tells customers an adult stands behind the business.
- **Social proof loop:** every buyer gets a message 5 days after delivery asking for a photo review. Offer 10% off the next order for a photo. Repost every one.
- **Secure checkout:** at launch, payment happens through trusted rails (Shopify Payments / PayPal / bank transfer to a parent's business account), never "send money to my personal card".
- **Packaging that looks premium:** kraft box, dust bag, tissue paper, sticker seal, handwritten card. This costs $2–4 and is what people film when they unbox.

### 1.4 Brand look & voice

- **Palette:** off-black `#111111`, cream `#F5F0E8`, cognac `#9A5B2E`, olive accent `#4B5320`.
- **Fonts:** a clean sans (Inter) + one serif for headlines (Fraunces or Playfair).
- **Voice:** short, confident, honest, a little funny. Never "luxury redefined". Yes: "Built to outlast your student loan."
- **Tagline options:** "Leather that grows with you." / "Made to be used. Built to last." / "Affordable luxury, zero fluff."

### 1.5 Product line at launch (keep it small)

Start with 4 SKUs, 2 colours max each. More choice = more stock risk.

| Product | Role | Colours |
|---|---|---|
| Minimal Cardholder | Entry product, impulse buy, gift, best margin | Black, Cognac |
| Messenger / Sling | Core product for students | Black, Dark brown |
| Everyday Backpack | Hero product (best video content) | Black, Cognac |
| Weekender Duffel | Premium anchor — makes everything else look affordable | Dark brown |

### 1.6 Pricing psychology

- **Anchor high:** the duffel at $189 makes a $119 backpack feel reasonable.
- **Charm pricing for small items, round for premium:** cardholder $29 (not $30), duffel $189.
- **Bundle:** Backpack + Cardholder = $139 (save $9). Bundles raise average order value (AOV), which is what makes shipping costs bearable.
- **Free shipping threshold:** set it just above your most popular product, e.g. free shipping over $100 → pushes messenger buyers ($99) to add a cardholder.
- **Launch price, not discounts:** "Founding customer price: $99 (regular $119) for the first 100 orders." Scarcity + a reason. Never run endless sales — it kills a premium brand.
- **Compare openly:** "Similar full-grain backpacks from big brands: $250–400." Only state comparisons you can back up.
- **Personalisation upsell:** initials hot-stamped for +$7 (a $40 stamping kit pays for itself after 6 orders).

### 1.7 Sourcing options

| Option | Startup cost | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Local leather workshop / craftsman** makes to your design | Low MOQ (5–20 pcs) | Fast samples, you can visit and film, best story | Higher unit cost |
| **B. Wholesale manufacturer** (Alibaba, 1688 via agent, or regional factories in Turkey, India, Pakistan, Italy outlets) | MOQ 20–100 pcs | Lowest unit cost, custom logo | 2–6 weeks lead time, quality risk, need samples first |
| **C. Make cardholders yourself** from leather offcuts | $60–120 for tools + leather | Amazing "behind the scenes" content, highest margin | Slow, only for small goods |

**Recommended:** C for cardholders (content + margin) + A or B for bags. Always order **paid samples** from 2–3 suppliers before any bulk order. Ask each supplier: leather type & grade, thickness, tanning (veg/chrome), thread type, hardware material, MOQ, lead time, logo embossing cost, defect policy.

### 1.8 Margin calculation

**Formula**

```
Landed cost  = unit price + (freight + import duty/fees) ÷ units + packaging
Variable cost per order = landed cost + payment fee (≈3% + $0.30) + shipping you pay + ad cost per sale
Contribution margin = selling price − variable cost per order
Target: landed cost ≤ 30–35% of selling price (≈3× markup)
```

**Example unit economics (USD, illustrative — plug in your real quotes)**

| | Cardholder | Messenger | Backpack | Duffel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unit cost from supplier | $5.50 | $28.00 | $38.00 | $58.00 |
| Freight + duty per unit | $1.00 | $5.00 | $7.00 | $11.00 |
| Packaging (box, dust bag, card) | $0.90 | $2.80 | $3.20 | $4.00 |
| **Landed cost** | **$7.40** | **$35.80** | **$48.20** | **$73.00** |
| Selling price | $29 | $99 | $119 | $189 |
| Markup (price ÷ landed) | 3.9× | 2.8× | 2.5× | 2.6× |
| Payment fee (3% + $0.30) | $1.17 | $3.27 | $3.87 | $5.97 |
| Shipping you absorb* | $4.00 | $0 | $0 | $0 |
| **Profit before ads** | **$16.43** | **$59.93** | **$66.93** | **$110.03** |
| Margin before ads | 57% | 61% | 56% | 58% |

\* Orders under $100 pay shipping (e.g. $6.90 flat). The cardholder column assumes you offer it with "free shipping in a letter envelope" ($4) as an impulse item. Bags over $100 ship free — the shipping cost ($8–15) is already inside the price; subtract it if you choose free shipping.

**Rules of thumb**

- If profit before ads on a product is < $15, don't run paid ads on it alone — sell it as an add-on.
- Max ad cost per sale (break-even CPA) = profit before ads. Target real CPA ≤ 40% of that.
- Set aside **10% of revenue** for returns, defects and taxes from day 1.

### 1.9 Starter budget ($500–$800 version)

| Item | Budget |
|---|---|
| Samples from 2–3 suppliers | $120–180 |
| First stock: 15 cardholders + 4 backpacks + 4 messengers + 2 duffels (or pre-orders, see Day 8) | $250–400 |
| Packaging (50 sets) | $50–70 |
| Phone tripod, ring light, white backdrop | $30–40 |
| Domain + first month of store | $15–40 |
| Tiny test ads / boosted posts | $0–70 (only after organic content proves itself) |

Cheapest path: **pre-orders**. Show the samples, take orders with a deposit or full payment for "ships in 14 days", then buy stock with that money. Be 100% honest about the ship date.

## 2. Technical & Sales Infrastructure

All code lives in [`site/`](site/). Open `site/index.html` in a browser — no build step.

```
site/
├── index.html      Mobile-first landing page: hero, trust bar, catalog, order sheet, FAQ
├── config.js       Your products, prices, WhatsApp number, Telegram username  ← edit this
└── order.js        Catalog renderer + cart + WhatsApp/Telegram checkout link generator
```

### 2.1 How the order flow works

1. Customer taps **"Order"** on a product card → it goes into a small cart (colour + quantity).
2. Customer taps **"Checkout"** → a bottom sheet asks for name, city, delivery method, and optional initials (personalisation).
3. Customer taps **"Send via WhatsApp"** or **"Send via Telegram"** → the script builds a pre-filled message with an order number, items, totals and details, and opens the chat with you.
4. You reply with the payment link (Shopify draft order / PayPal / bank transfer to the business account) and the ship date.

Why this flow: zero monthly cost, works on day 1, and the conversation itself builds trust (customers talk to the founder). Move to a full Shopify checkout once you're doing 5+ orders a day.

### 2.2 Setup in 10 minutes

1. Open `site/config.js`, set `whatsappNumber` (international format, digits only, e.g. `447700900123`) and `telegramUsername` (without `@`).
2. Replace product names, prices and image URLs. Put your own photos in `site/img/` and point `image` to `img/backpack-black.jpg`.
3. Host for free: drag the `site/` folder into Netlify Drop, or turn on GitHub Pages for this repo (Settings → Pages → folder `/site`). Connect your domain later.
4. Put the link in your TikTok/Instagram bio and Telegram channel description.

### 2.3 WhatsApp & Telegram catalog setup

- **WhatsApp Business app** (free): add a catalog with the same 4 products, set a greeting message ("Hey! I'm [name], founder of HIDE & HUSTLE. Send me your order and I'll reply within 2 hours ✌️"), quick replies for `/pay`, `/shipping`, `/care`, and labels: *New order → Paid → Shipped → Review asked*.
- **Telegram channel** as a catalog: one pinned post per product (photo, spec, price, "Order: link"). Post behind-the-scenes there too — it becomes your fan club.
- **Order log:** a Google Sheet with columns `Order ID | Date | Name | Items | Total | Paid? | Tracking | Review asked?`. Copy the order message into it every time.

### 2.4 Upgrading to Shopify (around order 30–50)

- A parent creates the Shopify account (Basic plan). Theme: "Dawn" or "Sense" — clean, free.
- Use the same product photos and copy. Install: product reviews app (Judge.me free), TikTok channel app (for pixel/catalog, parent-owned), Shopify Inbox.
- Keep the WhatsApp button on product pages — many young customers still prefer chatting before buying.

## 3. TikTok & Social Media Content Engine

### 3.1 The system

- **Post 1–2 TikToks per day**, and cross-post the same video to Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts (remove watermarks — export the original from CapCut).
- **3 content pillars**, rotated:
  - **A. Founder story** (POV / storytime) — builds the fan base.
  - **B. Product & craft** (making, packing, leather tests, ASMR) — sells.
  - **C. School + business life** (routines, numbers, lessons) — makes it relatable and shareable.
- **Every video:** hook in the first 1 second (text + motion), one idea per video, 9–25 seconds, ends with a reason to comment or follow.
- **Engagement:** reply to comments *with videos* (TikTok "reply with video") — these are your easiest viral wins.
- **Track:** views at 1 hour, average watch %, profile visits, link clicks. Remake any video that beats your median by 3× with a new hook.

### 3.2 Script 1 — POV storytelling

**Title:** "POV: you're 14 and just spent all your savings on leather"
**Length:** 18–22 s · **Pillar:** Founder story · **Goal:** follows + emotional connection

| Time | Visual (shot) | Text overlay | Voiceover / sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–1.5 s | Close-up: hands dropping a big roll of leather on a desk, *thud*. Quick zoom-in. | **POV: you're 14 and just spent ALL your savings on leather** | Hard drop sound, then music starts |
| 1.5–5 s | You looking at the camera, a bit nervous, phone in hand showing a bank app (blur the numbers) | "my mom: are you sure about this?" | — |
| 5–10 s | Fast cuts: cutting leather, stitching, a wonky first attempt, you laughing at it | "attempt #1 😬" → "attempt #7" | Music builds |
| 10–15 s | The finished cardholder sliding into a jeans pocket, then a slow-motion turn in natural light | "attempt #23" | Beat drop |
| 15–20 s | You holding the box, writing a handwritten note | **"Day 1 of building a leather brand at 14. Follow to see if I make it."** | VO (quiet): "First order ships tomorrow." |

- **Visual style:** natural window light, warm colour grade, handheld for story parts, tripod for the product shot.
- **Audio:** trending emotional/uplifting sound with a clear beat drop (search TikTok's "Commercial Music Library" for business accounts; pick a track used in < 50k videos for freshness). Keep the music at ~20% under any voiceover.
- **Caption:** "Building a leather brand at 14 — day 1. Would you buy this? 👀 #smallbusiness #teenentrepreneur #leathercraft #fyp"
- **Pinned comment:** "Link in bio if you want one of the first 100 🤍"

## 4. Step-by-Step Execution Roadmap: Day 1 → Day 30 (target: 100 sales)

**Realistic target math:** 100 orders × ~$70 average order value ≈ $7,000 revenue. Most orders will be cardholders and bundles at first. If you get 40–60 orders in month 1, that's a strong start — the system below is built to keep compounding into month 2.

### Red flags — stop and talk to a parent

- A supplier asks for full payment via an untraceable method (gift cards, crypto, personal transfer) with no samples.
- An "influencer" or "investor" asks to meet in person, asks for personal info, or wants you to pay to be featured.
- A customer insists on paying outside your normal payment link or sends "too much" money and asks for change back (classic scam).
- You're spending more than ~1.5 hours a day on the business on school days for more than a week — reduce posting, not sleep or grades.
